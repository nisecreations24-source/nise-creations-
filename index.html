Our Story

Made with love, right here in Danao City

Nise Creations by Shazny started in September 2024 as a small, home-based craft business — no storefront, just a table full of paper, ribbon, and wax, and a love for making things by hand.

What began with a few personalized stickers grew into a full line of custom keepsakes, event souvenirs, printing services, and hand-poured scented candles. Every order is still designed from scratch, never copied from a template — because we believe your celebration deserves something made just for it.

Today, families across Cebu trust us with their birthdays, baptisms, weddings, and everyday little joys. Free layout assistance comes with every order, because good design shouldn't be out of reach for a special occasion.
